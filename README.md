KPI DEFINITIONS
Net Sales = Quantity × Unit Price × (1 − Discount Rate)
Product Cost = Quantity × Unit Cost
Estimated Contribution = Net Sales − Product Cost − Delivery Cost
Contribution Margin = Estimated Contribution ÷ Net Sales

LIMITATIONS
Synthetic data only. Estimated contribution excludes overheads, taxes, returns, marketing, payment fees, inventory holding costs and other unrecorded expenses. It is not accounting profit.
